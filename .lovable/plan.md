# Shared technician conversation for nearby jobs

## What will change
- Add a conversation panel to every nearby repair job so approved matching technicians can read the full request discussion.
- After a technician sends the first offer, keep the conversation available and allow additional messages.
- Show messages from the customer and every interested technician together, clearly labelled without exposing private contact details.
- Keep the first-message action as “Send offer”; subsequent messages use the regular conversation send action.

## Access and safety
- Update message access rules so approved technicians whose selected repair categories match an open request can read its discussion.
- A technician may post only after expressing interest; the first offer creates that interest before adding the message.
- Preserve customer access and the existing assigned-technician conversation after a job is accepted.

## Verification
- Confirm an interested technician can send multiple messages.
- Confirm another matching technician can read the shared thread, express interest, and reply.
- Confirm unrelated or unapproved technicians cannot access the thread.
- Check the app compiles successfully after the changes.
